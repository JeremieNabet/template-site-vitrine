BMKY App

Flutter CRM app connected to Salesforce, built for the Beth Amidrash in my neighborhood.

This app manages contacts and donors, pledge commitments, payments, field actions, notifications and statistical tracking, with a smooth mobile experience for teams.

Back to projects

Overview

A mobile operational management platform with a simple journey: secure startup, 5-tab navigation and continuous synchronization with Salesforce.

User journey

SplashScreen, Firebase initialization, push permissions, FCM token retrieval then HomePage with Actions, Search, Stats, Notifications and PDF navigation.

Key modules

Event actions, contact search, full account view, annual analytics tracking, payment notification center and operational PDF generation.

Integrations

Salesforce REST + custom Apex, Firebase Messaging, Hebcal for Shabbat schedules and device services for call, SMS and email from contact records.

Features built

Actions

Event management, contact-linked action creation and soft-delete business logic for a clean history.

Search & Account 360

Account search, indebted account filter, outstanding amount review and detailed account/contacts/transactions view.

Stats & steering

Annual dashboard with monthly payment/donation aggregations and chart visualization to track activity in real time.

Notifications

Payment notification center, filters and read/unread marking to speed up daily processing.

PDF

Business document generation (Tofess 46 and Shabbat schedules) with sharing options and direct mobile export.
